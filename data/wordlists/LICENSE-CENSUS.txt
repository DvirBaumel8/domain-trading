data/wordlists/us-places.txt is derived from the U.S. Census Bureau 2026 Gazetteer, National Places file:
https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2026_Gazetteer/2026_Gaz_place_national.zip (retrieved 2026-10-06).

The Gazetteer is a work of the United States Government. 17 U.S.C. section 105 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/105):
"Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government, but the United States
Government is not precluded from receiving and holding copyrights transferred to it by assignment, bequest, or otherwise."

Only place names and USPS state codes are kept. Suffixes (city, town, village, CDP, borough, municipality, ...) are stripped,
names are lowercased and reduced to letters (Los Angeles -> losangeles). The Census Bureau does not endorse this derived file.

data/wordlists/given-names-census1990.txt (v5.56.0) is derived from the U.S. Census Bureau 1990 name files
https://www2.census.gov/topics/genealogy/1990surnames/dist.male.first and dist.female.first (retrieved 2026-10-26): the 600 most
frequent male and 600 most frequent female first names, lowercased, letters only, 3+ letters, merged.
data/wordlists/surnames-census2010.txt (v5.56.0) is derived from the U.S. Census Bureau 2010 surnames file
https://www2.census.gov/topics/genealogy/2010surnames/names.zip, Names_2010Census.csv (retrieved 2026-10-26): ranks 1-5,000,
lowercased, letters only, 3+ letters. Works of the United States Government (17 U.S.C. section 105, above). Used only by the
auction shortlist's PERSON_NAME filter (src/modules/ops/auctions/person-names.ts).
