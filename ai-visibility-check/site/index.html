Honest answers

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and nobody honestly can. AI assistants change their answers constantly. We measure how often you're named, show you what the businesses being named have in common, and help you fix what's in your control. Then we re-measure with the same questions.

Is this what I'd see if I asked ChatGPT myself?

Close, but not identical. Answers vary by person, location and time — identical lists repeat in under 1% of runs (SparkToro, January 2026). That's why we ask many questions several times and report a rate with a range, instead of one screenshot.

Which AI assistants do you check?

The paid report checks ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude with their web-search features turned on. The free quick check uses one AI model answering from memory only, so treat it as a first look.

What do the fixes involve?

Mostly the basics AI assistants read: complete and consistent profiles on the sites they cite for your trade (Google, Yelp, Healthgrades, Avvo and so on), clear service and city information on your website, and honest review requests to real customers. Studies link these to being named; none prove a guaranteed effect, and we say which evidence is stronger.

Do you post fake reviews or spam forums?

Never. Fake reviews are illegal under the FTC's rule, and undisclosed forum posting backfires. We only work with real customers and truthful content.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI, Google or Anthropic?

No. We're an independent service. Product names belong to their owners.

We use your business details to run your check and your email to deliver it. We don't sell data. See the privacy policy.
