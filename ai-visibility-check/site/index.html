How it works

AI answers change from one ask to the next, so a single screenshot proves nothing. We measure.

1

We ask what your customers ask

Dozens of real questions for your trade and city — “emergency dentist near downtown”, “best implant dentist in Austin” — asked to each AI assistant, several times.

2

We check the names

Where an official register exists (dentists, orthodontists, chiropractors), every business the AI names is checked against it, so you see real competitors — and names that don't match any registered business. For other trades the names are listed as the AI gave them and are not register-checked.

3

You get the why and the fixes

A one-page report: how often you're named, who's named instead, the likely reasons, and a prioritized fix list rated by how strong the evidence is.
