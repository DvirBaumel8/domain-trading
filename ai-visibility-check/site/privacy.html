Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Dvir Baumel (“we”, “us”) handles information if you use AI Visibility Check.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: business name, city and state, type of business, website, email address, names of competitors you list, and a payment reference.
  • Payment: payments are processed by Polar, our payment processor and merchant of record. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Technical data: the IP address and time of a form submission, and ordinary server logs, to run and secure the site.
  • Free check: if you run the free quick check, we store the details you entered so we can show or send the result.

How we use it

We use this information to run your check, deliver the report, answer your questions, keep records of orders, and improve the service. We do not sell your information. We do not use your email for newsletters or marketing unless you ask us to.

AI assistants

To produce a paid report we send customer-style questions about your business (for example, “best dentist in Austin, TX”) to third-party AI services such as OpenAI, Google, Perplexity and Anthropic. We do not send them your email address or payment details. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive. Answers from those systems vary and can include names of other businesses.

Who else can see it

We share information only with the payment processor, the AI providers as described above, and tools we use to host the site and email you — and only as needed to provide the service or as the law requires. We may also share a white-label report with an agency that ordered it for you, if you are their client.

How long we keep it

We keep order and report records for as long as needed to deliver the service, handle refunds, and meet tax and legal duties, then delete or anonymise them when they are no longer needed. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at the email below, unless we must keep it by law.

Where we are

The operator is in Israel. The service is offered to US businesses. Your information may be stored or processed in Israel, the United States, or the country of a provider we use.
