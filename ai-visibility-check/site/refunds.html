Refund policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy applies to the paid AI Visibility Check ($149 one-time) and to the add-ons (Maps, Listings, Content and the Fix-It Bundle). Everything below that says "Check" or "report" applies equally to an add-on or bundle deliverable.

If we don't deliver

If you paid for a Check, an add-on or the bundle and we do not email you the report within 5 business days after we have both your payment and a completed intake form (business name, city, type of business, and email), you get a full refund.

If we cannot run the paid AI assistants named in your order (ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, or the subset you chose), we will not substitute a free-model or memory-only result and call it the paid report. We will either complete the paid run within those 5 business days or refund you in full.

What we do not refund

We do not refund a delivered report solely because an AI assistant still does not name your business, or because a later ask gives a different list. AI answers change from one ask to the next; identical lists repeat in under 1% of runs (SparkToro, January 2026). We measure how often you are named and list likely fixes. We never promise a ranking or that a fix will change what an assistant says.

We also do not refund because you disagree with a finding, as long as the report was delivered as described and the method is shown in the report.
