## Failures and warnings
- **Andrew Rosener:** lost ~$300k on ~1,800 hand-registered brandables at $9-12 ("scratch tickets"); one $250-1,000 name beats 20 junk names.
- **Morgan Linton:** bought 1,200, dropped 600 as junk a year later.
- **priusdomains:** 300-400 non-.com names, 1 sale in 2 years, refused ~20 three-figure offers. Forum: ~100 .com names and 4-5 years of funding.
- **lknights1987:** 250 names, 0 sales in 7 months: prices too high ($5k+ vs $2-3.5k).
- **Nikul Sanghvi:** ~$250k in, ~$400k back over 9 years, only 3 profitable years; renewing out of sunk cost.
- **Auction wins can be reversed:** GoDaddy refunds a won expired auction when the old owner renews (seen up to day 29): treat a win as provisional for 30-45 days.
- **Lease-to-own (NameBio's 290 Afternic LTO deals, A):** 41% of finished deals cancelled, 36% of cancellations after one payment; a deal that reaches payment 2 completes ~70%; longer terms cancel more.

## Competition (who we bid against)
- HugeDomains and similar buy across GoDaddy expired auctions under ~$500 by machine: one bidder lost 150 auctions, 84 to HugeDomains. A tracking script saw HugeDomains win 10-20% of results; individuals 37-48%.

## Tools: "you can't succeed without X"?
Not supported. A 34-vote NamePros poll: 38% pay for nothing; free NameBio, DotDB, Google Trends, free ExpiredDomains.net cited as enough. Paid tools named by single sources only: NameBio paid (Cultra), EstiBot (rough guide; DNW's test of 15 appraisal tools: none accurate), Domain Discount Club Pro for Afternic search history (Allemann), paid contact data for outbound (one 6-month test: 20 names, ~600 leads, 6 sales $43k). "You need 1,000+ names": no source states it; several say ~100 good .com names is enough.

## Automation and AI
Builders exist (a 9-module vetting pipeline written with AI; an AI first pass over 30-50k expiring names a day; ML price models) but **none reports an AI edge in picking that turned into measured sales.** Proven gains from automation: speed, filtering, discipline, renew-or-drop. Mike Mann (BuyDomains, 350k names) reports revenue growth from data- and AI-driven pricing, not from more names.

## Synthesis for us
1. Expect 1-3% sell-through a year, most sales a year or more after the buy: at 40 names with a 2-year hold, about 1-3 sales in total. Small-sample results are noise: judge the method (replay, paper trading), not the score.
2. The winners buy at $11-70 and sell at $1,000-2,500 through Afternic/GoDaddy search (inbound, Buy Now). Our price band and channel match.
3. Profit is made on the buy and on renew-or-drop discipline, not on volume.
4. Our edge can be: selectivity (we can skip anything without proof of buyers, unlike HugeDomains), free automation of what others do by hand (the GoDaddy file, the Afternic API, the GoDaddy Auctions API), and fast checks. Not: out-bidding machine buyers on contested names, or "AI picks better" (unproven anywhere).
5. Open strategic questions for Dvir: (a) Allemann's 2026 shift to AI-assisted hand registrations vs our "Line 1 is the side line" rule; (b) lease-to-own 16 months vs the cancellation data (shorter terms complete more); (c) treat auction wins as provisional for 30-45 days in the books.
