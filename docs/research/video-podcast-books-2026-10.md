# Video, podcast and book round (DOM research session B, 25 Oct 2026)

Dvir asked for "more YouTube channels, famous YouTube channels regarding domain trading, maybe some books", graded and compared with our system. Knowledge file: `data/knowledge/0005-video-podcast-books.jsonl`: 8 new authors, 36 new sources, 13 new claims, 96 evidence rows, 9 links. It validates with 0 errors against 0001-0003 (the adapted `kb-gen/validate.ts`).

**How it was read.** 10 videos through YouTube's own "Show transcript" panel (auto captions) in a new tab of Dvir's Chrome. Read only: no likes, no subscribes, no comments, no sign-ins, nothing downloaded, no transcript services. The tab was closed at the end. Podcast show notes, platform blogs, book pages and reviews were read as ordinary pages. Pages that answered 403 (NamePros blog, Medium, Spaceship) were left out, not worked around. Every quote is at most 15 words and every excerpt is in my own words. Sources I could not read are not in the file. The exception is D-graded pages, which are kept for the record (weight 0).

**Big caveat.** None of these sources is about our exact case (40 S8 names, 2-year hold, $1,488-$4,888 Buy Now). Most of the weight is self-reported, or comes from marketplaces and brokers with a conflict of interest. Only our own bids and listings give direct evidence (finding F6).

## 4. Proposed action items

**Change now** (low risk, no founder rule touched):
- **C1. Lease-to-own on every listed name**, with a maximum term of 13-14 months at Afternic (and payment plans with a down payment where Atom is used). Evidence: 4 independent clusters put LTO at 25-36% of sales, with LTO sales averaging 35% higher. Decision: Dvir or Gavriel, at Afternic in Chrome. DOM confirms before any submit.
- **C2. GoValue is not comparable across its 2026 rebuild.** Any later use of the stored `valuation` (engine features, reports) must use only values from after the update. Note it in `gaps.md` with the engine feature list. Decision: DOM.
- **C3. Planning numbers for Gavriel's reports:** about 1.5% a year portfolio-wide, much lower for names in their first two years, and $2.5-5k median sale for small inbound portfolios. Show leads and views as the near-term measure (supports F15). Decision: DOM (report text).

**Reconsider when more data:**
- **R1. Uncontested auctions in the measurement.** Tag GoDaddy auctions with 0 bids ending within 72 h that pass the filters as a separate "uncontested" group (scored, never bid on automatically). Track their T30 outcomes like the pick-proof proxy, so auctions with bids, uncontested auctions and closeouts are compared on our own data. If uncontested names show the same or better proxy outcomes, bring the "≥ 1 bid" filter and the closeout pause to Dvir with that result. Decision: DOM builds the measure; Dvir decides any money change.
- **R2. Max-bid share.** Outside rules of thumb say about 10% of the expected end-user price (we use about 16%). Revisit after 10+ resolved bid plans: win rate, prices paid vs max, later leads. A lower share (12.5%) is the candidate. Decision: Dvir.
- **R3. Lander test for S8 names priced ≥ $3,488.** Afternic's own practice is request-price above about $2,500. Compare leads per name, Buy Now against request-price (with Buy Now shown after the survey), on half of those names. Decision: Dvir (pricing rule 4).
- **R4. Hold horizon (founder rule 3).** The evidence says most sales come after year 2-5. Before the first renewals, define what would earn a name a second renewal (for example, leads or views above a threshold, or a recent registration on another ending). Bring the founder-rule question to Dvir with our own view and lead data at the first renewal decision. Decision: **Dvir**.
- **R5. Capped API bidding.** Gavriel could place the plan's maximum through the GoDaddy Auctions API near the auction end instead of by hand (same plan, same approval, the max cannot be raised). This would come as a CR from Gavriel if wanted. DOM never places bids.

**No change:** founder caps ($250 per name, 40 names); the other-endings and shape signals (supported); valuation never scored (supported); the preset-max rule (supported); Line 1 behind the Tier A gate (supported); the BIN price list (supported).
