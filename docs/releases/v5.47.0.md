# Release v5.47.0 (25 Oct 2026): storage cut, adult filter, lift scoring in the shortlist, bot-aware visit counter

Contract MINOR. Migration `1767200000000_v5-47-0.sql`. Source: `docs/internal/loop/findings-actions-2026-10-25.md` F5, F8, F17, F18, F19, F20.

## What changed (contract)
MINOR (migration `1767200000000_v5-47-0.sql`: table `lift_models`, `visit_daily.suspect_hits`, `settings.closeout_expiry_keep_days`, `pickproof_pool_keep_days` 45 -> 1).
- New `GET /engine/lift` (READ or bots): the lift table (patterns, bins, lifts, counts, holdout AUC).
- New daily step `liftTrain` (after `engineTrain`): trains the lift table from the CR-002 seed and our own sales / unsold rows when they grew.
- `GET /auctions/shortlist`: `score_version` `auction-v3` when a lift table exists (name part from the lift, `parts.name.lift`, bids worth 5 points instead of 10); adult words and the company's forbidden terms are refused (`dropped.name_rules` `ADULT_TERM`, `FORBIDDEN_TERM`). The suggested-maximum rule is unchanged.
- `GET /pickproof/{date}/pool`: 410 `PICKPROOF_POOL_PRUNED` from the second day (was 45 days); the step `pickproofLabels` keeps only the arm names of older sealed days (`pool_rows_deleted_n`). Manifest, arms, hashes and labels unchanged.
- `buildPanel` `expiries` adds `regime_loaded`, `out_of_regime_deleted_n`, `decided_deleted_n`.
- `POST /visits/hit` accepts `ua_class: "suspect"`; `GET /visits` and `/visits/weekly` rows add `suspect_hits`; a name's human hits count at most 10 a UTC day, the rest are suspect.


## Impact on Gavriel
- Shortlist rows carry `auction-v3` and a `lift` block in `parts.name`; scores move (the bids part is half as big). Names such as `bestnaked.com` no longer appear.
- Do not re-check a pick-proof pool hash later than the day after the pick day through `GET /pickproof/{date}/pool`; rebuild it from the source files (`pickproof.md` recipe) instead.
- Read `human_hits` as an upper bound until the api-proxy Worker is redeployed with the new classifier.

## Expected storage after the change (live 25 Oct: 382 MB of 1 GB)
- `pickproof_pool` 57 MB (532k rows, growing about 14 MB a day) -> about 29 MB steady (2 pool days) plus under 5 MB a year of arm rows. Saves about 650 MB projected at 45 days. The migration rewrites the table (TRUNCATE + re-insert), so the disk is returned at once.
- `closeout_expiries` 21 MB (151,563 rows) -> under 5 MB (in-regime names only, decided rows deleted after 7 days); the migration drops the obviously out-of-regime rows and the next daily buildPanel the rest. Space is reclaimed by autovacuum reuse, not shrinkage: ask Gavriel to `VACUUM FULL closeout_expiries` once if the size matters.
- `lift_models`: a few KB per row, one row when the data grows.
- Net: about 350 MB expected after the next datasave run, instead of passing 1 GB within weeks.

## How to test
`GET /engine/lift` after the next `daily` run (model `source: seed`, `auc` about 0.79). `GET /auctions/shortlist` shows `auction-v3`. Check `/health` DB size after the migration.

**Lessons:** a test design that keeps every input forever (45 days of full 135k-row pools) is a storage bug waiting to happen; size every new table against the 1 GB limit when it is designed, not after it grows (G-133). The lift model reproduces the backtest (AUC 0.79 on the seed) only with a thin-bin rule: an empty bin must not get a free positive lift. Full suite: 277 files, 3,002 tests passed. The Worker redeploys by itself (its workflow runs on api-proxy changes).
