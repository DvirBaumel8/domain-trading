# Release v5.48.0 (26 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.48.0 (MINOR). The suggested maximum bid becomes the expected value of a name under our hold (F22, Dvir chose option (b) on 26 Oct 2026), and each shortlisted name shows its position in the day's field.

- **What changed:**
  - **Maximum bid = expected value.** Chance of a sale within the hold (10%) x a realistic net resale ($1,000 less 20% Afternic commission = $800) - one renewal ($11) = $69, so **$65 at full score**, scaled down by score as before. Was 20% of the net resale ($160). The 10% is about 3x the published rate for a 2-year hold (about 1.7% a year, round-3 research): it already assumes our picks sell better, and only our own outcomes may raise it (by migration). Settings: `shortlist_sale_chance_bp`, `shortlist_renewal_cents` (migration only).
  - **Fewer names pass:** an auction already at $65 or more has no headroom and is dropped.
  - **Position:** rows carry `field_n` (auctions that passed the filters that day) and `top_pct` (rank / field_n). While the lift table learns only from seed data the scores bunch at 90+, so "top 2% of 600" is the honest reading of the rank.
- **Impact on Gavriel:** when he shows Dvir a shortlisted name, the suggested maximum is now about $65 at most; above $50 he still shows why the name is worth more (founder rule 5).
- **How to test it:** `GET /auctions/shortlist` after the next data save: `settings.shortlist_sale_chance_bp` 1000, `renewal_cents` 1100; every `max_bid_suggested_cents` <= 6500; `top_pct` present on new rows. Tests AS545-4, AS548-1.
- **Deploy:** the Mac host after CI.
- **Lessons:** a maximum-bid rule must come from the expected value under our own holding rules, not from a share copied from investors with other rules (the professional 10-20% assumes 5+ year holds). Check a money rule against our founder rules before shipping it.
