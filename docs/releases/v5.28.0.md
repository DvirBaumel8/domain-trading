# Release v5.28.0 (21 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.28.0. CR-073 pick-proof mechanics. MINOR (additive). Migration `1766000000000_v5-28-0.sql`.

## What changed (contract)
- **Pick day D and the pool.** The pool of D is the sealed CR-071 save of the evening before (data date D-1, the 15:00 UTC run): `.com` closeouts of GoDaddy plus a sealed Dynadot closeouts upload, at most $40, minus sales-graded names, sorted by domain, one row per domain. It is frozen the first time anything asks for D (a pick or a manifest call): `pool_sha256`, and the seed = the first 8 hex digits of it. No sealed GoDaddy snapshot of D-1: 409 `SNAPSHOT_MISSING`.
- **Arms** (k = 100 a day, raise-only up to 300 by migration): **A** the Buyer's picks, **B1** random from the pool, **B2** random from the K1-passing names (stored closeouts `filter_code` null), **C1** random from names that are exactly two words, letters only, at most 10 letters. **C2 (.net-registered) is `not_built`**: the manifest and the report say so. Draws use the CR-008 recipe (MT19937, Fisher-Yates) with one derived seed per arm; a sub-pool with fewer than k names gives all of them and `short: true`.
- **`POST /pickproof/{date}/picks`** (bots or WRITE; `Idempotency-Key`; audited; non-money): `{names: [...]}`, at most k, replaces the earlier list until the day closes. A name outside the pool: 422 `NOT_IN_POOL` (the names are listed, nothing stored). From 16:00 UTC of D on, or once sealed: 409 `PICKPROOF_SEALED`.
- **`GET /pickproof/{date}/manifest`** (READ or bots): the seal. The first call at or after 16:00 UTC of D draws B1, B2, C1 and seals the day; later calls return the identical answer (pool sha256, seed, k, each arm's n and sha256, `recipe` hashes of the frozen draw, pool and label rules, `sealed_at`). The bots append it to `docs/requests/pick-proof-seals.md`; DOM commits no data.
- **`GET /pickproof/{date}/arms?arm=`** (READ or bots): the names of each arm, only after the manifest sealed the day (409 `PICKPROOF_NOT_SEALED` before), so nobody sees the random arms before picking.
- **`GET /pickproof/report?from=&to=`** (READ or bots): per arm n, events (taken), rate, `unknown_n`; A against C1 (primary: one-sided Fisher, alpha 0.05, plus CMH over the price bands $0-9.99, $10-19.99, $20-40), A against B1 and B2 (Holm-adjusted); the overlap of A with the other arm and, above 5%, a seeded within-day permutation test (`method: "permutation"`, 10,000 shuffles, reproducible) instead of Fisher; fewer than 10 events: `UNPOWERED`, no pass or fail. Every output is labelled "proxy, not profit".
- **New `datasave` step `pickproofLabels`** (ninth, last): T30 labels for the names of the sealed arms once the pick day is 30 days old: Verisign `.com` RDAP, one name at a time, at most 300 lookups a run. `taken` = RDAP registered and its status list has neither `redemption period` nor `pending delete` (any case or spacing, e.g. `redemptionPeriod`, `pendingDelete`), whatever the creation date (the creation date and `created_after_d` are stored for the Challenger and do not decide: a bought closeout keeps its old creation date); `not_taken` = registered in redemption period or pending delete (an unsold closeout lingers there, then disappears) or not registered (404); `unknown` = error or timeout (tried again until D+40, never read as not taken). **Changed from the CR as written:** only the names of the arms are labelled, not the whole pool; every name is labelled the same way with no arm attached. The step also prunes frozen pool rows older than 45 days (the sha256 stays for good).
- **Code:** the seeded MT19937 and `shuffle` moved from `selection/siblings.ts` to `src/core/mt19937.ts` (the bt1 sibling outputs are unchanged). New module `src/modules/pickproof/`. New settings (migration only): `pickproof_k` (a database trigger refuses a lower value), `pickproof_deadline_hour_utc`, `pickproof_pool_max_price_cents`, `pickproof_labels_max_per_run`, `pickproof_pool_keep_days`.
- Not in the backup (backup-coverage test states a reason per table); the admin-cli migration count is 63.

## Impact
- **Buyer:** posts its picks for D before 16:00 UTC of D with `POST /pickproof/{D}/picks` (names must come from the pool of the evening before, for example the rows of `GET /candidates/closeouts?date={D-1}`).
- **Challenger:** calls the manifest at or after 16:00 UTC, appends the answer to `docs/requests/pick-proof-seals.md`, then reads the arms; reads the report 30 days and more after the picks (the labels come from the daily job).
- **Gavriel:** nothing to do; the daily `datasave` run (15:00 UTC) has a ninth step. The report is a proxy, not profit.
- Nothing here asks Dvir to do anything.

## How to test (via the API)
- Before the deadline: `GET /pickproof/{D}/manifest` shows A as `open` and B1, B2, C1 as `pending_seal`; `GET /pickproof/{D}/arms` is 409 `PICKPROOF_NOT_SEALED`.
- `POST /pickproof/{D}/picks` with a name that is not in the pool: 422 `NOT_IN_POOL`; with real pool names: 200.
- At or after 16:00 UTC: the manifest seals; call it twice and compare (same `seed`, `sha256` values); then `GET /pickproof/{D}/arms?arm=B1` shows 100 distinct names from the pool; a new `POST .../picks`: 409 `PICKPROOF_SEALED`.
- `GET /pickproof/report` before any label exists: arms with `pending_n`, comparisons `UNPOWERED`.
- Local tests: PP528-1..6 (`tests/modules/pickproof/unit/stats.test.ts`), PP528-10..20 and 30..33, 40..43 (`tests/modules/pickproof/pickproof.test.ts`); `docs/internal/pickproof.md`.

Deploy: pushed to `main` with the tag; the Mac host deploys after CI passes. Gavriel confirms the live version on `/health`.

**Lessons:** a draw that others must be able to reproduce needs three frozen things: the input (the pool, stored with its sha256 because the source rows are pruned after 3 days), the recipe (hashed text in every manifest) and a seed derived from the input, never from a clock; and a label that is read per name and day, not per arm, keeps the test blind by construction. Moving a private helper (the seeded generator) into `src/core` instead of copying it kept one stream for the bt1 siblings and the draws.
