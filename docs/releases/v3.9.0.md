# Release v3.9.0 (10 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 3.9.0 (MINOR). Tech-debt pass over v3.0-v3.8. New `/report` warnings; no route changed shape.

## Contract changes
- `/report` warnings: **`PURCHASE_UNRESOLVED`** (warn; a real purchase `register_sent` or `unknown` for more than 30 minutes; details `purchase_id`, `state`, `age_minutes`, `created_at`) and **`DB_SIZE_HIGH`** (warn; database over 70% of Neon's free 0.5 GB; details `size_bytes`, `limit_bytes`, `percent`, `warn_percent`).
- **`POST_BUY_INCOMPLETE`** now means a `/buy` purchase with no screening pack (it meant "no comps"; comps are optional since pricing v3). Imported names are exempt.
- **`DROP_POLICY_FAILED`** (a 201 warning of 3.7.0) is removed: the drop date is set inside the booking transaction.
- **`POST /candidates/screen` with `domains`:** a name already screened on demand today is free and is accepted when the allowance is used up (202); `ON_DEMAND_SCREEN_CAP` stays for new names.
- **Reconciler (Dvir, 10 Oct 2026):** it never fails a `register_sent`/`unknown` purchase. Absent names stay open; `/report` shows `PURCHASE_UNRESOLVED`; DOM closes it with `resolve-purchase` after you (Gavriel) have checked the registrar account. When it books a purchase it now also turns auto-renew off and honours the request's `drop_policy`.
- **Jobs:** a step retried after a timeout that finds the job still busy is stored `failed` with `previous attempt timed out and may still be running`, not `skipped`.
- **Admin `import-domain`:** no comps needed while `comps_min` is 0.

## Impact on Gavriel
- Watch `/report` for `PURCHASE_UNRESOLVED`: look in the registrar account for the name and tell DOM (in `docs/requests/`): booked there (the reconciler books it by itself once the invoice shows) or never registered (DOM runs `resolve-purchase`).
- `DB_SIZE_HIGH` means storage is near the free limit: tell DOM.
- `POST_BUY_INCOMPLETE` after a `/buy` now means the name has no screening pack on file.
- Buy approval scope (Dvir, 10 Oct 2026): the buy approval line also covers the listing overrides passed with `/buy` (offer mode, pricing exception, custom floor or walk-away). No code change.

## How to test it (dry_run and reads only)
- `POST /buy` with `dry_run: true` still reports `drop_policy` and `drop_policy_line` as before.
- `GET /report`: the warnings above appear only when their condition holds. Nothing to run on production for the reconciler change; it is covered by tests.
