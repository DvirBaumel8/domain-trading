# Release v5.49.0 (26 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.49.0 (MINOR). From the 26 Oct improvement pass (`docs/internal/loop/improve-2026-10-26.md`, P1-P5).

- **What changed:**
  - **Storage (P1):** `closeout_expiries` kept 51,003 new names a day while 1,500 are checked (+9 MB a day). Now at most 1,000 new names a run, chosen by md5 (unbiased), and never-checked rows past the window are deleted; the migration trims to every checked row plus 15,000 sampled ones. `datasave_pool` keeps 2 days (was 3) and loses an unused index.
  - **Auction score v4 (P2, P3):** the name part is the name's position among the day's filtered auctions (the raw lift value bunched every top name at 95-99); the lift no longer reads the five own endings as the CR-002 sibling share; empty lift bins count 0; short split pieces not on a short-word list count half (insare, outswing). Headroom is a filter only: a $1 price with one bidder hides that bidder's maximum. New demand part from GoDaddy's free file fields: other endings with a live site (`developedTlds`) and exact-match registrations (`exactMatchTlds`).
  - **Paper trading (P4):** every served name and a random control of 25 a day are followed to their end; `GET /auctions/funnel` `paper` shows final prices and how many we would have won at our maximum. `would_win` is an upper bound when the last snapshot was before the final hours.
  - **Maximum bid (P5):** renewal $23 (GoDaddy's real price), so $55 at full score.
- **Impact on Gavriel:** shortlist rows read `auction-v4`; the reasons say "name in the top N% of today's field" and name the live endings. Maximums are $55 at most.
- **How to test it:** after the next data save, `GET /auctions/shortlist` (`score_version` auction-v4, `parts.demand`, spread of scores) and `GET /auctions/funnel` (`paper`); `GET /health` `db_size` for `closeout_expiries` (expected well under 10 MB). Tests AS549-1..14, AS545-4, AS548-1.
- **Deploy:** the Mac host after CI.
- **Lessons:** a feature must mean the same thing when a model is trained and when it scores (registered_share was 20 siblings in training, 5 own endings at serve time). And read every field of a file we already download before paying for lookups: GoDaddy's file had the demand signal we spent 1,500 RDAP lookups a day to approximate.
