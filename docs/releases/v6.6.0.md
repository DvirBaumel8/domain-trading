# Release v6.6.0 (4 Nov 2026)

**Contract:** 6.6.0. BUG-063 and CR-103 (both from DOM Architect). MINOR, no migration.

- **BUG-063 (fix):** Dynadot closeouts in `GET /candidates/closeouts` were never filtered or scored on the day they were sealed. The Dynadot steps run last in the datasave (5.65.0), after `scorePool`, so the day's sealed rows (and a CR-099 top-up) waited for the next day's run: digit names like `014229.com` were served with `NOT_SCORED_YET` and `filtered_by` all 0. Now the `dynadotCloseouts` step filters and scores the sealed Dynadot rows itself, right after its read, and reports it as `score_pool`. A bot upload sealed after the day's datasave still waits for the next run.
- **CR-103 (additive):** `GET /auctions/shortlist` explains the whole field: `field_n`, `stored_n`, `ended_n`, `prebid_dropped_n`, `not_stored_n`. Identity: `total + prior_kill_excluded_n + ended_n + prebid_dropped_n + not_stored_n = field_n`. Each row is counted once, in the order ended, pre-bid dropped, prior kill (DOM kept 6.4.0's meaning of `prior_kill_excluded_n`, instead of the CR's prior-kill-first order). `not_stored_n` covers the build's brand-typo drops, which are never stored. `GET /auctions/funnel` `shortlisted` adds `ended` and `prebid_dropped`.
- **Impact on Gavriel and the bots:** today's Dynadot closeouts are clean from the next datasave (15:25 UTC). The pack's "465 unexplained" rows now show up in the counts.
- **How to test it:** `GET /candidates/closeouts?source=dynadot` after the next datasave: `filtered_by.SPELL-1` above 0 and no `NOT_SCORED_YET` rows; `GET /jobs/runs` shows `score_pool` inside the `dynadotCloseouts` step. `GET /auctions/shortlist`: the identity holds on the newest date.
- **Deploy status:** pushed to `main` with tag `v6.6.0`; the Mac host deploys after CI.
- **Lessons:** when 5.65.0 moved the Dynadot steps last, nothing checked which later reads depend on their seal. A reorder of the datasave steps needs a "who reads this step's output" check. I first told Dvir the empty scores were by design, from the contract text alone, and was wrong for Dynadot: read the evidence (`filtered_by` all 0) before calling a bot's note harmless.
