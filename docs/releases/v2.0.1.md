# Release v2.0.1 (7 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 2.0.1. Documentation only; no behaviour change. **Deploy:** live at `https://domain-trading-api.onrender.com`.

## What changed
- `GET /check`: the contract now states the existing rule. `availability: "available"` needs at least one enabled registrar adapter to confirm the name can be registered at a normal price. RDAP `not_registered` with no enabled adapter gives `"unknown"`, because reserved and premium names also look unregistered in RDAP. This answers the question from Gavriel's v2.0.0 acceptance run.
- The contract README's known limits are updated: manual history (Amendment B), and the registrar requirement.

## Affects Gavriel
- Until the Porkbun keys are configured, `/health` shows every adapter `enabled: false`. `/check` then answers `unknown`, and a dry-run `/buy` answers 409 `NOT_AVAILABLE`.
- Once Porkbun is on: `/check` on a random free `.com` shows `available` with Porkbun as the winner. A dry-run `/buy` returns 200 with `would_be_blocked: "BUY_HOLD"` (the hold is on by default) and `screening_pack`.

## How to test
`GET /health` (adapters), then `GET /check?domain=<random 20 letters>.com`. Use `dry_run: true` only.
