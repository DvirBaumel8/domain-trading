# Release v5.59.0 (27 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.59.0 (MINOR, migration). CR-081: the historical replay replaces the pick-proof (Dvir 27 Oct; common decision DOM + Gavriel).

- **What changed:**
  - **Pick-proof off** (`settings.pickproof_enabled` false): `pickproofSeal` / `pickproofLabels` report `{status: "off", reason: "CR-081"}`; no new pool rows; a pick for an unsealed day is 409 `PICKPROOF_STOPPED`. Sealed days stay readable.
  - **Daily archive** on the host's disk: `godaddyInventory` writes `<ARCHIVE_DIR>/godaddy/<date>-<file>.jsonl.gz` (`.com` rows at or under the S8 cap, every field) for the biddable auctions and the closeouts; never deleted. `/health` `archive: {days_n, first, last, missing_days}`. 20-26 Oct were archived by DOM before this release.
  - **Replay** of an archived day: `POST /replay/runs` (WRITE) runs today's filters and score on that day's archived rows only (point-in-time: no later data, a lift table trained on or before the day), draws N random names from the same filtered field (seed from the archive's sha256, MT19937 + Fisher-Yates), and reports outcomes side by side with counts: auction final $100+, final at or above our maximum, retail sale after the day (when stored), closeouts taken/dropped (weak, pending until day + 30). Fewer than 20 positives = too small to decide; a seeded paired bootstrap of ours minus random; the names that did well, ours and the randoms we missed, with their features. Read with `GET /replay/{date}` (READ or bots) and `GET /replay/runs[/{id}]`.
  - **Conflict resolved (DOM):** the CR asked to freeze the logic as it stood on each day; past days' recipes were never stored, and Dvir asked to test and improve the current logic, so v1 runs `logic: "current"`; `as_of_date` is 422 `REPLAY_LOGIC_UNAVAILABLE` until recipe versions are stored.
- **Impact on Gavriel:** read replays with `GET /replay/{date}`; stop pick-proof routines (done on his side).
- **How to test it:** `POST /replay/runs {"date":"2026-10-22","kind":"auctions"}`, then `GET /replay/2026-10-22`. Tests RPY81-1..21.
- **Deploy:** the Mac host after CI.
- **Lessons:** a 30-day forward test cost more waiting than it was worth; saving every day's list and replaying it gives the same comparison within days.
