# Release v5.57.0 (26 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.57.0 (MINOR, behaviour).

- **What changed:** the auction score v4's name part is the lift (length + word count) alone; word quality no longer multiplies it and v4 has no uncommon-word cap. On Gavriel's D2 dev split (2,532 retail sales of $500+ vs 10,112 closeout leftovers) word quality scored AUC 0.47 and the lift alone 0.82, while the old product scored 0.62; on our own auction labels word quality was 0.48. Short brandables, which sell at retail, are no longer marked down.
- **Impact on Gavriel:** coined and brandable names rank higher; reasons no longer say "capped".
- **How to test it:** after the next data save, `GET /auctions/shortlist` (no "capped" reason in v4 rows). Test AS557-1.
- **Deploy:** the Mac host after CI.
- **Lessons:** a hand-built quality gate needs its own backtest; two independent label sets (auction demand and retail sales) both showed it near a coin toss.
