# Release v5.80.0 (30 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.80.0. MINOR, all additive:
- `POST /jobs/run` gains the optional `only` (a Dynadot re-pull);
- `/health.datasave_detail.dynadot_low_coverage`;
- the `/report` warning `DYNADOT_LOW_COVERAGE`;
- the Dynadot step summaries and manifests gain `coverage_status`, `coverage_ratio`, `stopped_by` and `top_ups`.

One migration (`1769200000000_v5-80-0.sql`, the seal guard).

## What changed (CR-099)
On 30 Oct Dynadot answered HTTP 500 from page 95 onward. Three failures in a row ended the read, so the aftermarket day was sealed with 91 of 477 pages.

- **Retry inside the step.**
  - A failed page waits 15, 30, 60, then 120 s (with jitter) and the read goes on.
  - Five failures in a row end a sweep, and one retry sweep follows.
  - Each read has a time budget: 50 min for the aftermarket, 25 min for closeouts.
  - The summary says why the read stopped (`stopped_by`).
- **LOW_COVERAGE.**
  - A day with under 90% of its planned pages read is sealed with `coverage_status: LOW_COVERAGE`.
  - The rule reads the page lists, so 30 Oct (91/477) already counts as LOW_COVERAGE.
  - `episodes` leaves such a day out, so a name missing from it never starts a new episode or looks gone.
  - `dqMarket` leaves it out of survivorship, price sanity and the coverage median, and fails `coverage` for it.
  - The Dynadot closeouts gone count in `buildPanel` uses full days only.
- **Same-day top-up.** A later read of a LOW_COVERAGE day asks only the missing pages and adds their rows to the same day. Rows, sha256 and coverage are recomputed; the seal time stays. A full-coverage day stays frozen.
- **Retry run.** A Dynadot step that ends LOW_COVERAGE or in error is retried once, 60 minutes later, by a datasave run of just that step.
- **Re-pull.** `POST /jobs/run {"job": "datasave", "only": "dynadot_aftermarket" | "dynadot_closeouts"}` re-pulls today's day of one source.
  - WRITE token only, audited.
  - 409 `SNAPSHOT_SEALED` when today's day has full coverage.
- **Closeouts pages past the end.** The closeouts pages are planned from the list's total. Pages 48–50 (29 Oct) and 59–61 (30 Oct) were exactly the pages after the last one (46,106 and 57,684 rows), and Dynadot answers those with HTTP 500. They are no longer asked, so they no longer count as failures.

## Deploy
Tag → Mac host. The migration only replaces the guard function. Pushed after today's datasave and outside the 23:45–01:00 UTC window.

## Lessons
"Stop after 3 failures" was written for a broken API. A server that drops out for a while needs a pause and a second pass instead. The closeouts "failures" were our own off-by-pages, visible only once two days were laid side by side.
