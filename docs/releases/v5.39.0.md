# Release v5.39.0 (25 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.39.0. Auction bid plans (CR-077): a pre-bid safety check, Dvir's approved maximum bid, the real result, and the S8 import now needs the plan. MINOR (additive), except the S8 import rule below (an S8 import without a bid plan was accepted before and is 409 `BID_PLAN_REQUIRED` now). Migration `1766600000000_v5-39-0.sql`.

## Why
Auctions are the main buying line (cap $250 a name). Until now the server saw an auction name only after it was bought: no safety or demand check ran before money was bid and nothing enforced the maximum bid. Request: `docs/requests/CR-077-auction-bid-plans.md`. Rules and test ids: `docs/internal/auctions.md`.

## What changed (contract)
- **`POST /auctions/prebid`** (WRITE or bots; Idempotency-Key; audited; stores its answer): `{domain, venue: "godaddy"|"dynadot"}` -> `{prebid_id, verdict: pass|flag|fail, checks[{id, result PASS|FLAG|FAIL|SKIP, reason, evidence}], manual_required[EXACT_STRING_BUSINESS, HIST_WAYBACK], endings{net,org,co,io,ai}, s8_max_price_cents}`. Checks: SPELL-1, BRAND-1, BIGCO-1, TM_KILL, TYPO-1, SURBL-1, WEB-RISK-1, RDAP_STATUS, SAME_NAME_ENDINGS, UK_COMPANY. A source that cannot be read is a FLAG, never a pass.
- **`POST /auctions/plans`** (WRITE only): Dvir's approved maximum bid. His `approval_ref` must name the domain and the maximum bid amount; the prebid must be of this name and venue, at most 72 h old, not `fail`; both manual checks confirmed with a note; above $50 `reasons.comparables` or `reasons.other_endings`; `kind: "closeout"` is 409 `CLOSEOUTS_PAUSED` (new setting `s8_closeouts_allowed`, false). One open plan per name (409 `BID_PLAN_EXISTS`).
- **`POST /auctions/plans/{id}/result`** (WRITE only): `{outcome: won|lost|withdrawn, final_price_usd?, ended_at}`. A won price above the plan maximum is 409 `PRICE_ABOVE_PLAN` and nothing is recorded.
- **`GET /auctions/plans`** (filters `status`, `domain`, `limit`) and **`GET /auctions/plans/{id}`** (with the event history) for READ or bots. An open plan whose auction ended more than 7 days ago reads as `expired`.
- **`POST /portfolio/import` (lane S8):** needs an `open` or `won` plan for the name (409 `BID_PLAN_REQUIRED`) and `price_paid` at or under its `max_bid` (409 `PRICE_ABOVE_PLAN`). With a plan, `approval_ref` may be left out: the plan's approval is used (`approval_from_plan: true` in the answer). A given `approval_ref` is validated as before. The plan becomes `won` with the paid price in the same transaction. A dry run reports `would_be_blocked: "BID_PLAN_REQUIRED" | "PRICE_ABOVE_PLAN"`.
- New error codes: `CLOSEOUTS_PAUSED`, `PREBID_REQUIRED`, `PREBID_FAILED`, `BID_PLAN_EXISTS`, `BID_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, `BID_PLAN_CLOSED`, `PRICE_ABOVE_PLAN`, `BID_PLAN_REQUIRED`.
- Tables: `prebid_checks` (append-only), `bid_plans`, `bid_plan_events` (append-only); all three in the backup.

## Impact on Gavriel
- Before proposing a bid to Dvir: `POST /auctions/prebid`, read the flags, do the two manual checks (exact-phrase business, history), then show Dvir the name, the reasons and the maximum bid.
- After Dvir's line (it must name the domain and the maximum in dollars): `POST /auctions/plans` with the `prebid_id` (made within 72 h). After the auction ends: `POST /auctions/plans/{id}/result` (win, loss or withdrawn, with the final price when known). This is the record of what auction names really cost.
- A won name is imported with `POST /portfolio/import`; the plan must be open or won. A plan left open more than 7 days after the auction end is expired: record its result first.
- Closeouts are refused until the pick-proof test shows an edge.

## How to test (via the API, dry runs and no money)
- `POST /auctions/prebid {"domain": "<a registered .com>", "venue": "godaddy"}` -> 201 with `verdict`.
- `POST /auctions/plans` with a fake approval for a test name: 422 `APPROVAL_INVALID` when the line does not name the maximum; 409 `PREBID_REQUIRED` without a recent prebid.
- `POST /portfolio/import` with `dry_run: true` for a name with no plan: 200 with `would_be_blocked: "BID_PLAN_REQUIRED"`.
- Local tests: AB539-1..24 (`tests/modules/ops/auction-plans.test.ts`). Full suite: 261 files, 2,872 tests passed locally.
