# Release v5.6.0 (14 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.6.0. CR-056 part 3: engine health (drift per year and quarter, ablation without endings, bars report)

- **What changed:** `GET /engine/health` now carries the rest of CR-056's health report: `drift` (how the sold and dropped names change per year and in the newest full quarter), `ablation` (the model retrained without the ending features, on the same frozen split) with `bar4_pass`, and the bars report (`bars` with six entries, and `bars_passed` with the ids that pass). Drift and ablation are computed during each training and stored on the model's metrics, so health reads them cheaply; they appear after the next training (a model trained under 5.5.0 shows `drift: null`, `ablation: null`, bar 4 `not_measured`). No new route, no migration. Contract: `endpoints.md` §GET /engine/health.
- **Impact on Gavriel:**
  - **Bars 1 and 5 are yours.** They are always `not_measured` with a detail text: bar 1 (health green for 3 days including a restart) and bar 5 (`POST /score` deterministic and under 5 seconds for your 20 fixed names). The service cannot know either.
  - **Bars 2, 3, 4, 6 are measured.** Bar 2: at least 10,000 sold and 10,000 unsold stored. Bar 3: test AUC at least 0.80 and precision at the top 1% at least 0.90 on the newest quarter, **and** at least 3 unsold per sale in the test file (a thinner mix fails the bar whatever the AUC). Bar 4: AUC without the ending features within 0.05 (absolute) of the full model; the detail also shows the precision at a 1:100 base rate with and without. Bar 6: the active model's test AUC against the prototype's 0.850 (the detail also names 0.809, the prototype's point-in-time trend reference); the prototype's split differs, so this one is indicative.
  - **Reading drift.** `drift` has one entry per year present in the sold data (`"2024"`, `"2025"`, `"2026"`) and `last_quarter`. Each has `window`, `sold` and `dropped` (null when no dated dropped row falls in the period). Blocks hold `n`, `net_pit_share` (share with a `.net` registered before that row's own date), `two_word_share`, `median_length`, `com_date_known_share`, `handreg_share` (null when the `.com` date is known for under 30% of the rows) and, for sold only, `median_price_cents` plus `median_price_by` (net yes/no, two/three words, handreg/other). Every row's features are as of its own sale or observation date. Compare years: a falling `net_pit_share` or a moving `median_price_cents` means the market the model learned from is shifting. Sold = every stored sale (not only the $100+ regime rows used for training); dropped = `dropped_after_held` not caught by a catcher.
- **How to test it:**
  1. `GET /engine/health` before any model: `drift`, `ablation`, `bar4_pass` are null, `bars` has six entries (1, 3, 4, 5, 6 `not_measured`; 2 `fail` below 10,000 of each), `bars_passed` is `[]`.
  2. `POST /engine/train`, poll until `done`, then `GET /engine/health`: `drift` by year and `last_quarter`, `ablation {auc_without_endings, auc95, delta, p_top1_at_1pct_base, removed, features_kept}`, `bar4_pass`, and the bars with their `value` and `detail`. The same `ablation` and `drift` are in `GET /engine/train/{id}` under `metrics`.
  3. Run your bar 1 and bar 5 checks and record them yourself.
- **Deploy:** no migration. Push to `main`, tag `v5.6.0`; Gavriel confirms the live version on `/health`. Training takes one more GBM fit (the ablation) and a pass over every stored row for the drift, so a training takes somewhat longer; the 25-minute budget still applies.
