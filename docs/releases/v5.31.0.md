# Release v5.31.0 (21 Oct 2026)

**Contract:** 5.31.0. CR-073a: a pick-proof day freezes only after the daily filter step (POOL_NOT_READY). MINOR (additive). No migration.

## What changed (contract)
- **New 409 `POOL_NOT_READY`** on `POST /pickproof/{date}/picks`, `GET /pickproof/{date}/manifest` and `GET /pickproof/{date}/pool` when they would freeze day D while some GoDaddy closeout rows of D-1 have not been through the daily filter step. `details: {date, data_date, pending_n}`. Nothing is stored; retry after the daily save of D-1 finishes (it starts 15:00 UTC; the filter step follows the download within minutes).
- **Why (Gavriel's `passes_k1` question):** every row of 2026-10-21 has `passes_k1: false`. Expected for that day only: its rows were saved on 20 Oct, before the filter step existed (v5.26.0 shipped 20 Oct 23:15 UTC), and the pool froze the next morning before the 21 Oct run processed them. The same could happen any day if a bot froze D+1 between the download and the filter step. The guard closes that gap. Day 2026-10-21 stays as frozen.
- Dynadot rows are not checked: an upload sealed after the daily run is filtered only by the next run, so a Dynadot row can still show `passes_k1: false` for that reason.

## Impact
- **Gavriel, Buyer, Challenger:** call pick-proof for day D after the daily save of D-1 is done; on 409 `POOL_NOT_READY`, wait and retry. B2 is meaningful from 2026-10-22.
- Nothing here asks Dvir to do anything.

## How to test (via the API)
- After the 21 Oct 15:00 UTC save: `GET /pickproof/2026-10-22/pool`: 200, and some rows have `passes_k1: true`.
- During the 15:00 UTC run, before the filter step: `GET /pickproof/2026-10-22/manifest`: 409 `POOL_NOT_READY` (only if nothing froze the day before).
- Local test: PP531-1 (`tests/modules/pickproof/pickproof.test.ts`).

Deploy: pushed to `main` with the tag; the Mac host deploys after CI passes. Gavriel confirms the live version on `/health`.

**Lessons:** a value derived from another step's output must not be frozen before that step ran; freeze-on-first-use needs an explicit readiness check, not just "the snapshot is sealed".
