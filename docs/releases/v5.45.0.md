# Release v5.45.0 (25 Oct 2026): the daily auction shortlist and the weekly funnel (G-127)

Contract MINOR. Migration `1767000000000_v5-45-0.sql` (table `auction_shortlist`, six `settings.shortlist_*` columns).

## What changed
- **First knowledge base load (CR-078):** `data/knowledge/0001-questions.jsonl` (25 decision questions) and `0002-october-research.jsonl` (8 authors, 203 graded sources, 93 claims, 271 evidence rows, 22 links), loaded by the daily step `kbLoad`. Test KB543-24 loads them. Tests use an empty knowledge folder by default.
- **Suggested maximum bid (DOM's review):** 20% of a realistic net resale (`settings.shortlist_expected_resale_cents`, default $1,000 from the knowledge base's end-user price band, less the Afternic commission), scaled by the auction score (full from 70), at most the S8 cap, rounded down to $5. The builder's draft used our list price (the S8 ladder BIN) and gave every name the $250 cap.
- Datasave step `auctionShortlist`: filters the in-range auctions (name rules, owned, open bid plan, brand typo against the stored list, room under the maximum bid), scores them (auction score v1, rules in `docs/internal/auctions.md` "Shortlist"), asks the other five endings of the top 50 whose answers are missing or older than 30 days (RDAP, 2-minute budget), stores the top 25 per data date, and runs the automatic pre-bid for the top 10 that do not fail (stored in `prebid_checks`, valid 72 hours). A FAIL name is kept in the table with its reason and not served.
- `POST /auctions/prebid` now shares its code with the step (`ops/auctions/prebid-store.ts`); its answer is unchanged.
- Retention step also deletes shortlist rows older than 90 days. `auction_shortlist` is excluded from the backup (derived daily data).

## Impact on Gavriel (a daily routine)
1. After the 15:00 UTC datasave run, read `GET /auctions/shortlist` (rows are in rank order; ended auctions and failed pre-bids are already left out).
2. Show Dvir 3 to 5 names with the suggested maximum bid and the reasons (and any `prebid.flags`; a `flag` verdict needs a human read, and the manual items, exact-phrase business search and Wayback, are still done by hand).
3. After Dvir's line (his approval names the domain and the dollar maximum), create the plan with `POST /auctions/plans`, passing `prebid.id` as `prebid_id` (valid until `prebid.valid_until`; a new pre-bid can be made with `POST /auctions/prebid` if it has expired).
4. Weekly: read `GET /auctions/funnel` and report the counts to Dvir.

## How to test via the API
`GET /auctions/shortlist` and `GET /auctions/funnel` only read. To fill the table, run the daily job `datasave` (Gavriel operates it); `GET /jobs/runs?job=datasave` shows the step summary (candidates, dropped counts, endings asked, pre-bid verdicts).
