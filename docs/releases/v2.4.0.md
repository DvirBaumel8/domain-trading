## AC-6: DOM's word split vs the research split: 1,767 of 1,900 agree (93.0%)
- **The count:** measured with production's word lists. The 133 differences are in `v2.4.0-ac6-split-differences.tsv` (domain, DOM's split, the research split).
- **The cause:** mostly DOM picking a rarer split, for example `balls tart` / `ball start`, `apps hipping` / `app shipping`, `ai stockport folio` / `ai stock portfolio`.
- **The effect:** those names get siblings built from the other words, so their share is measured on a different, usually odder, sibling set.
- **Under the 95% line in CR-008 §7, so this goes to Dvir.** DOM's recommendation is to **accept DOM's split as a known limit** for now:
  - **It is deterministic,** and nobody chooses it.
  - **It affects about 7% of names.**
  - **Buying stays paused.**
  - **Gavriel can compare** any name with `?domain=` and `?tokens=`.

  Improving the splitter (word frequencies) would need a new word list and a new method version.
