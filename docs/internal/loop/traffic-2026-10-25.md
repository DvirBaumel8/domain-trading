# Traffic and job timing, 12-25 Oct 2026 (Dvir: "investigate our traffic")

Read-only: the Mac host's `api.log` (12 Oct 22:21 to 25 Oct 14:36 UTC) and `GET /jobs/runs` with DOM's READ token.

## API calls
- **3,997 calls in 13 days.** 2,695 are `POST /visits/hit` (the lander visit counter) and 421 are `GET /health`. The bots made only about 900 calls.
- **Every call takes about 1 second**, even `GET /health` (1,058 ms average). Most of that is the trip from the Mac to the Neon database and back, repeated per query. So **every database round trip costs real time here**, and batching matters more than on a server next to its database.
- **Wasted bot calls:** many calls failed on input, so the bots retried.
  - `/buy`: 20 of 41 got a 422, 7 a 400.
  - `/visits/forward`: 26 of 51 got a 422 and 15 a 429.
  - `/portfolio/import`: 12 of 30 got a 422.
  - Rate limit (429): about 40 times in all.
  - `/health`: 61 calls without a token (401).
- **Slowest route:** `POST /selection/sibling-scan`, 27 s average and 81 s at most (the 80 s budget; G-122).

## Daily jobs (latest runs)
- `daily`: about 35 s for 19 steps. `datasave`: about 2 minutes (GoDaddy download 73 s, `buildPanel` 20 s, `scorePool` 14 s).
- **They are fast only because almost no data flows yet:** `engineEndings` asked 5 pairs (the cap is 20,000), disappearance tagging made 25 lookups (cap 1,500), pick-proof labels had nothing due, and `POST /data/sales` was never called. **The row-by-row paths of G-121..G-124 are idle today. They become slow exactly when the historical data is loaded**, so the bulk fixes must ship before any bulk data load.
- `snapshotRetention` deleted 124,762 pool rows on 24 Oct. So `datasave_pool` stays at about 3 days (about 140 MB) and does not grow (G-119).

## What we can't see yet
How many database queries each call or job step makes. The log has only the total time. A query counter per request and per job step (count and DB milliseconds, written to the log and the step summary) would show every N+1 directly from real traffic (proposed, G-125).
