## What worked
- **Turnaround:** 10 BUGs and 24 CRs in a week, most built the day they arrived. CR-098 (knowledge load blocked three nights) and CR-099 (Dynadot low-coverage day) were each fixed, deployed and checked against production within a day.
- **Reading production directly** (DOM's READ token since 25 Oct) found the DB growth, the 0008 clash, the Oct 30 low coverage and today's outage without waiting for a bot.

- **The one-writer rule for the knowledge base** (6.0.0) closed the source of three failed nights.

## What didn't
- **The outage (G-138).** Neon free gives each project **100 CU-hours of compute and 5 GB of data transfer out a month**; using either up suspends the database to the month end ([Neon plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans)). We watched only storage. Two causes from the code and logs (which quota actually ran out is still to be read by Gavriel on the Neon console):
  - **Data out:** `episodes` reads all of `market_names` (468k rows, 354 MB) back to the Mac on every datasave; a Dynadot top-up reads the whole day back. A month of that is several GB.
  - **Compute:** the database sleeps only after 5 idle minutes. The Mac's visits-token check connected every 10 minutes; `/health` polls (52 on 30 Oct) and DOM's own `/jobs/runs` pollers (147 on 30 Oct) did the rest. Estimated about 9 hours awake a day, plus the datasave at higher compute.
  - The cost: 31 Oct has no Dynadot day (it can't be re-read), the visit counter and every route failed for 8 hours, and nobody was alerted. The 00:05 UTC run cleared the stuck step and ran the aftermarket read after midnight (dated 1 Nov).
- **DB storage is on course for the 1 GB limit around 8 Nov.** `market_names` is 354 MB (heap 246 MB, indexes 108 MB) because the daily fold rewrites nearly every row.
- **Request status drift:** about 10 CR/BUG files still say `approved` or `open` though DOM's response says shipped (G-140).
- **Code outside the release flow:** two changes (BUG-056/057, CR-088) arrived as Cursor Agent pull requests that Dvir merged. They worked, but they skipped the code map, the evidence file and DOM's review. DOM picked them up in later releases.
- **Bot pain points:** not asked this week, so none came. Asked again today.
