## Findings (verified), ranked by money x confidence / effort

**P1. Storage leak: closeout_expiries grows without a limit** (efficiency D1; DOM's own read). `ops/closeouts/expiries.ts:53-67` keeps every in-regime name (51,003 on 26 Oct) while lookups stop at the 1,500 cap; open rows live 120 days. About +9 MB a day, about 1 GB at steady state: on its own it fills Neon free in weeks. **Action:** collect at most about 1,000 a day by a deterministic md5 sample (unbiased labels), delete never-checked rows past the end window, one-time rewrite to the newest sample. Also `datasave_pool` keep 3 -> 2 days and drop its unused `(source, domain)` index (about 50-70 MB). DOM.

**P2. The auction score can't tell names apart, so the bid ladder is flat** (buy side B1, B2; money A5). Verified `shortlist.ts:48-53`:
- the lift's `registered_share` was trained on 20 sibling names (CR-002) but is served as the share of the name's own five endings, and both lift features come from the same five lookups: one registered ending gives the capped +2 twice, so top names land at 95-99 "sold-like";
- headroom gives every $1 auction 19.7 of 20 points: a single bidder's hidden maximum is invisible, so $1 means "uncontested so far", not cheap (68% of 1-bid $1 names stayed at $1, 13% reached $65+, 22-26 Oct sample);
- result: 22 of 24 names get the same $65.
**Action:** serve `registered_share` as unknown; headroom becomes a filter, not points; the name part is the name's position in the day's field; thin lift bins outside the seed's range (1 word, 4+ words) count 0, not the worst lift; short split pieces ("ins", "are", "outs") count half. DOM.

**P3. Free demand data in the GoDaddy file is thrown away** (buy side B3). Verified in today's `all_biddable_auctions.json`: `exactMatchTlds`, `developedTlds` (other endings with a live site: our strongest signal, `signal-live-businesses` moderate), `keywordRegistrations`, `semrushSearchVolume`, `semrushCpc`. Nothing in `src` reads them. In 706 in-range auctions the share reaching $100+ rose from 9% (1-2 exact-match endings) to 59% (6-14). **Action:** store them in `godaddy_auctions_live` and the engine rows; use them in the score instead of 50 slow RDAP lookups. Free, every name, no lookups. DOM.

**P4. Nothing measures whether the shortlist or the $65 would win anything** (money A1, buy side B5, research F Q2). Outside sources can't answer "can we win at the opening price"; our own data can in weeks. **Action:** paper-trade: store the end state (last price, bids) of every shortlisted name plus a random control of 25 a day; show "would have won at our maximum" and the scores of those names in `/auctions/funnel`. Note: the once-a-day snapshot (14:08 UTC) misses the last hours, so add the next day's file / RDAP check for the final state. DOM.

**P5. The maximum bid uses an $11 renewal; GoDaddy renews at about $23** (money A2). Verified: `report.json` renewal $23.19 (mineunited), $23.00 (promptinjectionaudit). 10% x $800 - $23 = $57 -> **$55**. Research round 4 (knowledge file 0007): the evidence-central 2-year sale chance is about 5% (range 3-8%); 10% is the optimistic edge, acceptable only as the ceiling for the best names. **Action:** renewal 2300; with P2 the maximum scales by position, so a median name gets about $20-30. DOM (inside Dvir's rule (b)).

**P6. Engine labels compare two extremes** (buy side B4). "Sold" = an auction whose last snapshot price was $100+; "unsold" = a closeout nobody took at $5. About 77% of contested in-range auctions (ending $1-$99) get no label, and all our candidates sit in that middle. A model trained on it calls almost everything sold-like. **Action:** label ended in-range auctions by final band ($1 / $5-99 / $100+) inside one population; keep closeout drops as a separate "no demand" class. DOM, after P1-P4.

**P7. The main line has 10 slots, the side line 30** (money A3). Verified `report.json` lines: Line 1 `max_names` 30, S8 10. At about 2-5% sold a year, time to the first sale falls with the number of names held (5 names: years; 40 names: months). **Action:** move the split (e.g. S8 30 / Line 1 10) by migration. **Dvir** (caps).

**P8. 15 of 18 warnings are noise** (sell side C5, reliability E1-E4). Verified: NS_UNVERIFIED x3 and REGISTRAR_DRIFT x3 both come from the stale stored Afternic nameservers of the three Porkbun names that forward through the Worker (`visits/store.ts:58-60` says so); FLOOR_AUTO_ACCEPT x5 fires for every hybrid name by design (`warnings.ts:252`); REVIEW_OVERDUE fires while the review is disabled (`warnings.ts:101-105`); DATASAVE_MISSED and DROP_FEED_STALE expect bot uploads while the bots are paused. Real ones: EXPORT_PENDING/STALE (mineunited, lease-to-own mismatch, P9) and AUTO_RENEW_UNCONFIRMED x2. **Action:** fix the data and the rules so the list is about 5 real items. DOM.
