1. **Zero buys from a filtered batch is normal.** A May 2026 GoDaddy Closeout workbook of **57** names after quality filters still ended **Ready to buy: none**.

   Same thread. Supports CR-088 / Dvir “no artificial cut after hard filters.”

3. **1%–2%/year sell-through is still the adult number.** Sully Nov 2025 and Brad Feb 2026 both say experienced investors are happy with ~1–2% inbound STR on reasonably priced names; **50** decent names can go a year with **zero** sales and that is normal.  
   [Sully](https://www.namepros.com/blog/are-we-misleading-new-domainers-about-their-chances-of-success.1369670/) (B) · [Brad holding costs](https://www.namepros.com/threads/holding-costs-the-silent-portfolio-killer.1376460/) (B)

   Brad holding-costs thread.

   [Hawkes](https://www.namepros.com/blog/is-godaddy-appraisal-value-a-useful-filter.1297561/) (B) · [GoValue](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/news/godaddys-domain-value-appraisal) (A)

## Implications for hard filters + cheap-first + CR-088

**CR-088 (full hard-filter field, no top-N cut)** is aligned with how volume people actually work: ingest the day’s inventory, decide fast on thousands, expect most rejects and few wins. Our ~500–750 hard-filter passers/day is **small** vs GoDaddy’s 50k+/day expired adds and vs fka’s 2,500 decisions.

**Cheap-first order that matches published workflows:**
1. Free machine Layer A (extension, price band, bids, ending window, brand/typo lists) — keep **all** passers.
2. Free Buyer shape: ≤2 words, spellable, not coinage junk, not personal-name siren, GEO needs wide buyer pool.
3. One-sentence buyer/use/resale thesis — kill if you cannot write it.
4. Active-use / Exact Match TLD / Developed TLD as cheap demand proxies (platform now gives these).
5. Only then: live page / USPTO / paid evidence.
6. Appraisal = triage/competition hint, **never** a hard min or max for Layer A.
7. Every run: top-5-with-cons report; buy cards only after Risk Gate. Log unfinished judgments — do not pretend names disappeared.

**Resource save without cutting the field:** batch RDAP/endings/prebid on a **top slice for expensive steps only**; store/serve the full ranked field. That is infra scale (queues, indexes), not discovery amnesia.
